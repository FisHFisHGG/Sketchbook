3. What stays on your device

  • Your notebooks are always stored in your browser on your device (IndexedDB). Free accounts never upload them.
  • Settings such as the theme are stored in your browser (localStorage). These are strictly necessary for the app to work, so no consent banner is needed. We don’t use cookies for tracking or advertising.
  • A notebook PIN or password is stored only as a scrambled check value (a hash), never as plain text. Fingerprint unlock on Android uses your phone’s own fingerprint reader; your fingerprint never leaves your phone and we never see it.
  • Fonts and libraries are part of the app, so opening Foliet does not contact font or code services. Signing in contacts Google’s sign-in service.

4. Who processes data for us

  • Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd.): sign-in (Firebase Authentication) and the database (Cloud Firestore, located in the EU – Frankfurt). Google may process some data outside the EU under the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe Ltd.): payments, once paid plans are on sale.
  • Hosting: GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) and, after launch, Netlify, Inc. They serve the website and may keep short technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security.

We don’t sell your data, and we don’t share it with advertisers.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account, usage facts, survey answer and cloud notebooks: until you delete your account.
  • Live links: until you press “Stop sharing”, delete that notebook’s link or delete your account.
  • Feedback messages: up to 2 years, then deleted.
  • Records of used promo codes (only a technical ID and the code): kept to stop codes being reused.
  • Payment records: as long as tax and accounting law requires.

6. Security

Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database access is limited by security rules: you can only read your own data, and even we cannot open your notebooks from the admin panel.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, have it corrected, deleted or limited, take it with you (portability), object to processing based on legitimate interest, and withdraw consent at any time. In Foliet:

  • Download my data (menu → Account) gives you a file with everything we keep about your account, plus your notebooks.
  • Delete my account (menu → Account) deletes your account and all its data from our servers right away.

For anything else, write to [CONTACT EMAIL]; we answer within one month. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Romania: ANSPDCP, dataprotection.ro).

8. Children

If you are under 16, please ask a parent or guardian before creating an account.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top. If a change matters for you, we’ll also tell you in the app.
