Questions

What is Foliet Sketchbook?

A digital sketchbook and notebook that opens in your browser. Draw, write, plan and stick things down on pages that look and turn like paper. Create a free account and start on your phone, tablet or laptop.

Is it really free?

Yes. The free plan gives you 2 notebooks with up to 20 pages each, every drawing tool, stickers, notes and text, and locked notebooks. Pro adds unlimited notebooks and pages, PDF import and export. There are no ads on any plan.

Where are my notebooks saved?

Always on the device you use, so they open even without internet. Each browser keeps its own copy, so use Foliet from the same browser and the same web address. With Pro they’re also saved to the cloud, so the same notebooks open on your phone and your laptop, and they come back if a device loses them. You can also download a backup from Settings at any time.

What if I delete a notebook by mistake?

It goes to Recently deleted, at the bottom of your shelf: for 30 days with Pro, or 2 days on the free plan. Tap Restore and it’s back with all its pages.

Can I organise my notebooks?

Yes. Drag them into the order you like (on a phone, hold a notebook for a moment, then drag), and give them tags to find them fast. Tap ⋯ on a notebook to rename it, tag it or move it. With Pro, you can also make your own categories, like Study or Travel: each one is a tab above your notebooks.

Can I keep a notebook private?

Yes, on every plan. Lock a notebook with a 4-digit PIN or a password, and on Android you can also unlock it with your fingerprint. Anyone using the app on your device needs the PIN to open it.

Does it work on my phone or tablet?

Yes, in any modern browser. It works with touch and with a stylus, and it reads pen pressure when your device provides it.

What’s in the store?

Templates, cover designs, name labels and sticker packs. Lots are free: 8 templates (weekly planner, daily agenda, to-do lists, math grid, Cornell notes, journal, storyboard, sheet music), 16 covers, 6 label shapes and 6 sticker packs (7 stickers each). The rest – like a habit tracker, budget, recipe book, a galaxy or art deco cover, a kraft tag or gold foil label, and packs of cute animals, space and sweet treats – cost €0.99 each on any plan. Buy once and use it as often as you like. Some items are only around for a few days, so look in now and then. Paid items go on sale together with Pro.

Can I share a notebook?

Yes. Share → “View-only file” works on every plan. With Pro you can also make a live link, like foliet.app/trip-to-rome/k3x9qa: anyone who has it can look, without an account, and it changes as you draw. If you allow it, people with Pro can sign in and draw and write in it too, and their changes come to your notebook. Stop sharing whenever you like.

Can I import a PDF?

With Pro, up to 500 pages per PDF. The first page becomes the cover, and every other page is fitted onto a notebook page so you can write on top of it.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled whenever you like, and yearly plans simply don’t renew. Nobody is charged yet, because paid plans haven’t launched.

What happens if I go back to the free plan?

Your notebooks stay. You just can’t add more pages or notebooks beyond the free limits.
