A notebook with weight

A thick cover with a stack of pages behind it. Choose the cover color, then draw or put a photo on it.

Pages you turn by hand

Drag a page and it follows your finger. You see its edge as it turns, then it settles flat.

Drawing tools that behave

Pen, pencil, a brush that thins as you move faster, marker, highlighter, shapes and fill. Each tool remembers its size, and opacity is one slider away.

Words, stickers and notes

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The free plan gives you 2 notebooks with up to 20 pages each, every drawing tool, stickers, notes and text. Pro adds unlimited notebooks and pages, PDF import and export.

Where are my notebooks saved?

Today, in your browser on the device you use. Cloud sync is coming, so you can open the same notebooks on your phone and your laptop. Until then, clearing your browser data would remove them.

Does it work on my phone or tablet?

Yes, in any modern browser. It works with touch and with a stylus, and it reads pen pressure when your device provides it.

Can I import a PDF?

With Pro. You choose portrait or landscape, and each PDF page is fitted onto a notebook page so you can write on top of it.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled whenever you like, and yearly plans simply don’t renew. Nobody is charged yet, because paid plans haven’t launched.

What happens if I go back to the free plan?

Your notebooks stay. You just can’t add more pages or notebooks beyond the free limits.
